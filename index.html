Origin

The pattern behind everything

Physician-trained thinker, metaphysical author, and founder of ITOSHIRO ZUNA LLC. The work lives where science, spirit, geometry, and story refuse to stay in separate rooms.

A living thesis

Nothing is random. Time does not only move forward — it spirals. Lessons return at a higher octave. Patterns repeat until they are understood. Across books and games, Zuna maps how attention becomes identity, how emotion becomes transformation, and how imagination participates in reality.

The visible and invisible dimensions of life are connected through pattern. See the pattern, and you can choose coherence over noise.

One house. Four voices.

ITOSHIRO ZUNA LLC now carries four imprints: Itoshiro Zuna for metaphysics and visionary fiction, I Z for children’s stories, KAI for books written by his son, and BeiBei Rollings for dark-humor satire.
